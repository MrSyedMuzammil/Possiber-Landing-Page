FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does Possiber do?

It runs on top of YouTube in your browser and strips away recommendations, comments, Shorts, and autoplay. You build playlists of the videos you want to learn from, and Possiber plays through them cleanly.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and Edge. It installs as a browser extension from each browser's store.

What are the plans and what do they cost?

Free gives you up to 2 playlists stored locally, with the full distraction-free player. Pro ($5/month or $42/year) unlocks unlimited playlists and syncs across all your devices. Lifetime is a single $195 payment that includes Pro and all future updates.

How does billing work?

Pro subscriptions are handled by Paddle, our payment provider. You can pay monthly or yearly. Paddle manages your invoices and payment details. We never see your card.

Do you collect my data or watch history?

No. We never collect your browsing or watch history. On the free tier, your playlists live only on your device. On Pro, we store your email and subscription status, nothing more.

How do I cancel Pro?

Cancel anytime from your account. Your subscription stays active until the end of the billing period, then drops back to free. Your playlists aren't deleted.

Does Possiber access my YouTube account?

No. Possiber only cleans up the pages you're already viewing and plays embedded videos. It doesn't log into or read anything from your YouTube account.

How do I get support?

Email us anytime at the address in the footer and we'll help you out.

What are coins and how do I earn them?

Coins are rewards for completing Pomodoro focus sessions. The longer you focus, the more you earn. A 30-minute session earns 30 coins, a 60-minute session earns 70. Stop early and you lose some.

What is the Dashboard?

The Dashboard is a full-screen view of all your playlists. Switch between grid and list view, search every video, toggle progress dots, hide watched lessons, and sync with YouTube.

What are Collections?

Collections let you organize playlists into folders. Physics, Chemistry, Side projects. They help you keep things tidy when your library grows. Pro feature.

What themes are available?

Free gives you Dark and Light. Pro adds Paper (warm cream tones) and Midnight (deep dark with neon accents). Switch anytime from Settings.
